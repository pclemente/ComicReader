Open up your comics on iPhone Duo — and enjoy new ways to open your next read.

• Keep your page and zoom as Duo opens and closes, with artwork and controls adapting to the display.
• New on Mac: open PDF, CBZ/ZIP and classic CBR/RAR files directly from Finder without importing. Choose Add to Library when you want a copy with saved progress and optional iCloud sync.
• Browse your preferred comic websites, save shortcuts and keep website sessions between visits. Private browsing and website-data controls are included.
• New with Plus: download supported comics into your library with file validation, duplicate detection, collection selection and a download queue.
• Plus also includes comic text search, saved quotes, reading lists, named bookmarks and image filters.

Duo support, direct Mac reading, browsing, ordinary file imports and iCloud sync are free. Plus is a one-time purchase with Restore Purchases, not a subscription. Existing Plus owners get the new tools. Websites must provide supported downloadable files; no comics are included.
