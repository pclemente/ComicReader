Open your own comics on Mac.

ComicReader reads PDF, CBZ/ZIP and classic CBR/RAR files. Open a comic directly from Finder or build a library for saved progress, organisation and optional iCloud sync. Reading, ordinary imports, browsing, current-page text recognition and iCloud sync are free.

OPEN STRAIGHT FROM FINDER
Right-click a supported file and choose Open With > ComicReader, use File > Open Comic or press Command-O. Read it in its existing folder without importing. Direct reading does not save progress after the file is closed; choose Add to Library when you want a library copy with saved progress and optional iCloud sync.

BUILD YOUR LIBRARY
• Import PDF, CBZ/ZIP and classic CBR/RAR files with familiar system tools, the local-network uploader or WebDAV.
• Organise comics into collections and sort them by name, date or reading status.
• Read imported comics offline, zoom into artwork and keep each library comic's read status and current page.
• Recognise text on the current page on your Mac, or use embedded PDF text when available. Select, copy and share the result. Recognition quality varies with language, scan quality and lettering.

OPTIONAL ICLOUD SYNC
Choose whether to sync library comic files, read status and current page across Mac, iPhone and iPad. Sync stays off until you turn it on and uses your personal iCloud Drive storage. Sufficient storage and an internet connection are required.

BROWSE ON YOUR TERMS
Visit websites in ComicReader, save shortcuts, keep a normal website session between visits or use a separate private session. Browsing and website-data controls are free. Websites must provide supported downloadable files; sign-in, availability and downloads depend on each site. ComicReader includes no comic catalogue, bundled comics or DRM removal.

COMICREADER PLUS — ONE OPTIONAL PURCHASE
Plus adds tools for readers with larger libraries:
• Website Imports download supported files into a chosen collection with validation, duplicate detection and a visible queue. Keep ComicReader open until imports finish.
• Comic Search & Quotes builds a persistent text index for the current library comic, searches indexed pages and saves editable quotes linked to their source page.
• Reading Lists & Smart Queues arrange a reading order without moving files and gather Continue Reading, Unread and Read Again.
• Named Page Bookmarks return to memorable pages.
• Image Filters provide Clear Ink, Grayscale and Warm Paper display options without changing the original file.

ComicReader Plus is one non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. The App Store shows your local price before purchase. Restore Purchases is available on Mac, iPhone and iPad with the same Apple Account. Existing Plus owners keep access to the current Plus tools.

Search covers the current library comic, not the whole library. Search indexes, quotes, lists and bookmarks stay on each device and are not included in iCloud sync. Saved quotes remain readable and shareable if Plus is no longer active.

On iPhone Duo, ComicReader keeps the current page and relative zoom as the display changes, with pages and controls adapted around the active fold. Duo support is free.

ComicReader is based on ComicFlow and is open source under GPL v3. Bring your own comics; screenshot sample comics are not included.
