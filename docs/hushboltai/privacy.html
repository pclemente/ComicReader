HushboltAI & your data

Privacy policy

Your device generates the answer. Your conversation is saved locally unless you choose a feature, such as private iCloud sync or export, that sends a copy elsewhere.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Conversations and storage

HushboltAI runs text generation on your device, using an included model or Apple’s on-device model when available. It does not send prompts or replies to a developer-operated server or a model provider. There is no HushboltAI account to create. Your conversations are not sent to the developer for model training.

The local library saves conversation titles, messages, dates and reply details such as which model answered. The app marks its library folder for exclusion from system backups. A separate, size-limited cache can save completed answers on this device to reuse for matching requests. It does not sync through HushboltAI. You can clear it or switch it off.

Private iCloud sync is optional

If your version supports sync and you enable it, HushboltAI sends the conversation library to your private iCloud database so your devices can share it. Apple operates iCloud. Synced conversations count against the storage in your own iCloud account. The developer does not provide a shared storage pool. The app saves an opaque account reference on your device to avoid mixing libraries after you switch iCloud accounts. There is no public cloud conversation directory or developer chat dashboard. Neither model files nor the reply cache are synced by this feature.

Delete a chat from the conversation library to remove its local contents. If you previously synced the chat, keep sync enabled on the same iCloud account and let a sync complete to remove its cloud contents. A deletion identifier without the chat text is kept to prevent an offline device from adding it back. Devices that are offline may retain their copy until they sync. Turning sync off stops future syncing; it does not delete cloud copies. Apple’s own storage and backup policies apply.

Voice and shared files

If you tap the microphone on a supported device, the app asks for microphone and speech-recognition access. It requires on-device transcription and does not save microphone recordings. Words you send become part of the conversation. Apple’s system voice can read a reply aloud. You can turn off these features or revoke microphone and speech access in system settings.

If you export a conversation or copy its text, you control where it goes. A file saved to a shared or cloud folder, or text sent to another app, follows the settings and policies of that destination. Deleting the chat in HushboltAI does not delete exported copies.

Free replies and purchases

HushboltAI keeps recent local dates and counts of completed free bundled-model replies on this device, in app preferences and a device-only Keychain entry. These counts are separate from conversation text and are not sent to a developer server or included in chat sync. A device-only counter can survive reinstalling the app.

Apple handles prices, payments and refunds through the App Store. HushboltAI checks Apple-signed transactions to find out whether Plus is unlocked. The app has no developer-operated purchase server and does not receive your payment card details.

Analytics and support

The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. Apple may process store activities or diagnostics according to your Apple settings. Read Apple’s privacy policy for its services.

If you contact support, only send information you want the developer to receive. The external contact form is hosted by WordPress.com; when you submit it, the name, email address and message you enter are used to respond to your request. See Automattic’s privacy policy. Using support is optional and is separate from using the app to chat.

Contact and privacy requests

HushboltAI’s developer is Pablo Clemente Pérez. To ask a privacy question or request access, correction or deletion of details you sent to support, contact the developer. To remove a conversation, use the library and sync controls; the developer cannot retrieve your local library for you.
