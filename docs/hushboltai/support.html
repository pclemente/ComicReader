Help for iPhone and iPad

HushboltAI support

Find an answer below or contact the developer. Your chat history is not needed to diagnose most problems.

Getting started

Open a new conversation and type a question or a short passage to rewrite. The models come with the app. Text chat works offline and needs neither a HushboltAI account nor an API key.

Common questions

What is free, and when does it reset?

Each device gets 10 newly generated, completed replies from the included models per local calendar day. An interrupted or failed reply does not count. Reusing a saved reply does not count either. Replies from Apple’s on-device model are unmetered when it is available. The app chooses the model for you.

What does HushboltAI Plus unlock?

Plus is a one-time purchase for this edition. It unlocks unlimited replies from the included models and Markdown export on supported platforms. It is not a subscription. The App Store shows the price for your region before you confirm.

I bought Plus. How do I restore it?

Open the Plus screen and choose Restore purchases while connected to the App Store. Use the same Apple Account that bought this edition. For a billing issue or refund, contact Apple about the purchase.

Why is the app large, or the first reply slow?

The two included models account for about 1.85 GB before the rest of the app. The first reply can take longer while a model loads. Leave the app in front to let it answer. For a quicker reply, turn off Longer replies and try one short question at a time. HushboltAI cannot look up live information, and generated answers can be wrong.

Can I dictate or listen to a reply?

On iPhone and iPad, tap the microphone and allow Microphone and Speech Recognition when prompted. Dictation needs an on-device recognizer for your language; if it is unavailable, you can type instead. In Settings & privacy, turn on Speak replies to listen. Send after a pause is optional; when off, dictation stays in the message field until you tap Send.

How do I delete a conversation or clear the cache?

On iPhone or iPad, open the library, choose the conversation’s actions menu (…), and confirm Delete conversation. This also clears saved reply-cache entries. To keep your conversations but forget previously cached answers, open Settings & privacy → Clear saved replies. Set Reply cache to Off to stop caching.

I used private iCloud sync. How do I remove a synced chat?

In a version with Sync with iCloud enabled, delete the conversation while signed in to the same iCloud account. Keep sync on and use Sync now while online. Other devices receive the deletion when they next sync. Switching sync off or uninstalling the app does not erase an existing iCloud copy.

Contact support

For a technical problem or privacy request, use Pablo Clemente Pérez’s developer contact form. Include “HushboltAI”, your device, system version, and what happened. You do not need to send your conversations, Apple Account password, or payment details.
