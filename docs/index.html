The clear version

A few useful answers.

Still stuck? Contact support.

Does ComicReader include comics?

No. You bring your own supported files and choose any websites you visit. ComicReader has no catalogue, bundled comics or DRM removal.

What does iCloud sync?

If you opt in, your library comic files, read status and current page sync using your personal iCloud Drive storage. Sufficient storage and an internet connection are required.

Does Plus search my whole library?

No. Plus indexes and searches the current library comic. Search indexes, quotes, lists and bookmarks stay local to each device and are not included in iCloud sync.

Is Plus a subscription?

No. Plus is one non-consumable purchase: €9.99 at the Spain base price, with local pricing shown by the App Store. Restore it on your devices with the same Apple Account.
